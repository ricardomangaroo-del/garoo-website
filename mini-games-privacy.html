Privacy Policy

Last updated: September 23, 2026

Garoo Interactive ("we," "us," "our") makes Garoo Interactive Mini Games (package com.garoo.pathline) — a compilation of 20 arcade, puzzle, and sport mini-games. This page explains, plainly, what the app knows, what it doesn't, and the choices you have either way.

The short version: Garoo Interactive Mini Games has no accounts or login, and does not collect, transmit, or share any personal information about you or your device — with anyone, including us. The app does offer optional real-money in-app purchases (coin packs and game unlocks), processed through Google Play Billing and Apple's In-App Purchase system (StoreKit). The only network connections the app makes are the ones those platform payment systems require to complete or restore a purchase you initiate — the app still has no analytics, no advertising, no accounts, and no other internet connectivity of any kind.

Information we collect

We do not collect any personal information. The app has no user accounts, no login, no analytics, no crash reporting, and no advertising. We have no way to identify who is playing, and no data about your play ever reaches Garoo Interactive or any third party. The one exception — covered in full below — is that the app connects to the internet solely to process in-app purchases through Google Play Billing (Android) and Apple's StoreKit (iOS); that is not general internet access, and it is not used to collect analytics, advertising, or tracking data of any kind.

Data stored on your device

To make the game work, the app saves your progress locally on your device only, using your device's standard app storage — never sent anywhere. This includes things like:

  • Your coin balance and which skins/cosmetics you've unlocked
  • Best scores, streaks, and progress for each of the 20 mini-games
  • Settings — volume, accessibility options (e.g. Race Assist), and similar preferences

Coins can always be earned for free by playing. Coins and individual game unlocks can also optionally be purchased with real money — see the next section for exactly how that works.

In-app purchases and network connections

The app offers optional real-money in-app purchases — coin packs and individual game unlocks — processed through Google Play Billing (Android) and Apple's In-App Purchase system, StoreKit (iOS). These are the platforms' own first-party payment systems; Garoo Interactive does not run its own payment service and never sees your payment details (card number, billing address, etc.) — those are handled entirely by Google Play or Apple, under their own privacy policies.

When you choose to buy something in the Shop, or use the "Restore Purchases" option, your device connects to Google's or Apple's payment servers to complete or verify that purchase. This is the only reason the app connects to the internet. It is not general internet access, and it is not used for analytics, advertising, or tracking of any kind — the app still has none of those.

The app only stores, locally on your device, whether you own a given item (for example, that a specific game is unlocked) — the same kind of local flag it already uses for coins earned through play. We do not receive or store any purchase history, payment method, or transaction data.

Purchasing is always optional: every game and every coin-priced item can also be unlocked for free by earning coins through play, with no purchase required.

Third-party services

The app does not include any third-party advertising networks, analytics platforms, or social features. The only outside service the app connects to is the platform billing system described above (Google Play Billing / Apple StoreKit), used solely to process purchases you initiate — it is not a tracking or data-collection SDK. Beyond that one, narrow exception, nothing about your device or play activity is shared with anyone, because nothing else leaves your device.

Children's privacy

Garoo Interactive Mini Games is a general-audience app and is not directed at children. It collects no personal data from anyone, of any age. If you believe a child has been targeted by this app in a way that concerns you, contact us using the details below.

Your choices

Because everything lives only on your device, you are always in full control of it: clear the app's storage or uninstall it to erase all local progress. There is no account to delete and no server-side copy of anything to request removal of, because none exists.

Changes to this policy

We'll update this page if the app's data practices ever change — for example, if a future version adds analytics, ads, or cloud save — and any such change will be described here, with a new date at the top, before it ships.
