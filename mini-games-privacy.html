Privacy Policy

Last updated: August 26, 2026

Garoo Interactive ("we," "us," "our") makes Garoo Interactive Mini Games (package com.garoo.pathline) — a compilation of 20 arcade, puzzle, and sport mini-games. This page explains, plainly, what the app knows, what it doesn't, and the choices you have either way.

The short version: Garoo Interactive Mini Games runs entirely on your device. There's no account to make, no login, and it does not connect to the internet for any purpose — nothing about you or your device is ever collected, transmitted, or shared, with anyone, including us.

Information we collect

We do not collect any information. The app has no user accounts, no login, no analytics, no crash reporting, no advertising, and makes no network connections of any kind. We have no way to identify who is playing, and no data about your play ever reaches Garoo Interactive or any third party.

Data stored on your device

To make the game work, the app saves your progress locally on your device only, using your device's standard app storage — never sent anywhere. This includes things like:

  • Your coin balance and which skins/cosmetics you've unlocked
  • Best scores, streaks, and progress for each of the 20 mini-games
  • Settings — volume, accessibility options (e.g. Race Assist), and similar preferences

Coins are earned by playing — the app has no real-money purchases and does not use Google Play's billing system.

Third-party services

The app does not include any third-party SDKs — no advertising networks, no analytics platforms, no social features. Nothing is shared with anyone because nothing leaves your device.

Children's privacy

Garoo Interactive Mini Games is a general-audience app and is not directed at children. It collects no personal data from anyone, of any age. If you believe a child has been targeted by this app in a way that concerns you, contact us using the details below.

Your choices

Because everything lives only on your device, you are always in full control of it: clear the app's storage or uninstall it to erase all local progress. There is no account to delete and no server-side copy of anything to request removal of, because none exists.

Changes to this policy

We'll update this page if the app's data practices ever change — for example, if a future version adds analytics, ads, or cloud save — and any such change will be described here, with a new date at the top, before it ships.

Contact us

Questions about this policy? Email us at hello@garoointeractive.com.
