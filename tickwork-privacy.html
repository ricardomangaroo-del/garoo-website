Privacy Policy

Tickwork (package com.garoo.tickwork) — published by Garoo Interactive

Effective date: 3 September 2026

This Privacy Policy explains how the app Tickwork ("the App," "we," "us") handles information. The App is a single arcade game — spin the dial, bank the ball — with three play modes, a selectable ball and arena roster, and an in-game currency ("Notches"), all running entirely on your device.

The short version: the App does not have accounts or a login, does not connect to the internet for any purpose, and does not collect, transmit, or share any information about you or your device — with anyone, including us.

1. Information we collect

We do not collect any information. The App has no user accounts, no login, no analytics, no crash reporting, no advertising, and makes no network connections of any kind. We have no way to identify who is playing, and no data about your play ever reaches Garoo Interactive or any third party.

2. Data stored on your device

To make the game work, the App saves your progress locally on your device only, using your device's standard app storage (never sent anywhere). This includes things like:

  • Your Notches balance and which balls/arenas you've unlocked
  • Best scores, lifetime stats, and Roster Milestones you've logged
  • Your selected ball, arena, and play-mode preferences
  • Settings — volume, accessibility options, and similar preferences

Notches are earned by playing — the App has no real-money purchases and does not use Google Play's billing system.

3. Third-party services

The App does not include any third-party SDKs — no advertising networks, no analytics platforms, no social features. Nothing is shared with anyone because nothing leaves your device.

4. Children's privacy

Tickwork is a general-audience app and is not directed at children. It collects no personal data from anyone, of any age. If you believe a child has been targeted by this app in a way that concerns you, contact us using the details below.

5. Your choices

Because everything lives only on your device, you are always in full control of it: clear the App's storage or uninstall it to erase all local progress. There is no account to delete and no server-side copy of anything to request removal of, because none exists.

6. Changes to this policy

We'll update this page if the App's data practices ever change — for example, if a future version adds analytics, ads, or cloud save — and any such change will be described here, with a new effective date, before it ships.

7. Contact us

Questions about this policy? Email us at hello@garoointeractive.com.
