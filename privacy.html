Privacy Policy

Last updated: July 26, 2026

Garoo Interactive ("we," "us," "our") makes Matter MazeBall, a mobile game. This policy explains what information the app collects, how we use it, and the choices you have.

Our default position is to collect as little as possible. Matter MazeBall runs entirely on your device and doesn't send any personal data anywhere.

Information we collect

We may collect:

  • Gameplay data stored locally — progress, settings, and preferences saved on your device. This never leaves your device unless the app explicitly offers cloud save.
  • Basic analytics — anonymous, aggregate usage data (e.g. session length, level reached, crash reports) used to improve the game. We do not use advertising identifiers or build cross-app profiles.
  • Contact information you provide — if you email us for support, we keep that correspondence to respond to you.

Information we do not collect

  • We do not sell personal data.
  • We do not use third-party advertising trackers by default.
  • We do not knowingly collect personal data from children.

How we use information

Any data we do collect is used only to operate, maintain, and improve Matter MazeBall — fixing bugs, balancing difficulty, and understanding which features are actually used. We do not use it to build advertising profiles.

Your choices

  • Most gameplay data can be cleared by clearing your device or browser's local storage for the app.
  • You can contact us at any time to ask what data we hold about you or to request deletion.

Data retention & deletion

Locally stored gameplay data persists only on your device until you clear it or uninstall the app. Support correspondence is retained only as long as needed to resolve your request. To request deletion of any data associated with you, contact us using the details below.

Children's privacy

Matter MazeBall is rated Everyone and is not directed at children under 13. We do not knowingly collect personal data from children. If you believe a child has provided us with personal data, contact us and we will delete it.

Changes to this policy

We may update this policy as our products evolve. Material changes will be reflected here with an updated date at the top of the page.
