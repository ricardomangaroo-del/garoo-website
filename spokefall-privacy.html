Privacy Policy

Spokefall (package com.garoo.spokefall) — published by Garoo Interactive

Effective date: 3 November 2026

This Privacy Policy explains how the app Spokefall ("the App," "we," "us") handles information. The App is a single puzzle game — turn rings, match colours, and clear the spokes before the wheel fills — with several play modes, a daily puzzle, an optional ad, and a settings screen.

The short version: the App has no accounts or login, and we do not collect your name, email, location, contacts, or anything else that identifies you. We run no server that receives your play data. Your scores and settings stay on your device. The game is free and has nothing to buy. It can offer you one optional ad, and it uses Google's advertising software (Google AdMob) to do that. That software starts with the game on every iPhone, iPad and Android device that can show ads, and can collect the technical information in section 3, even if you never watch an ad.

1. Information we collect

We do not collect personal information about you. The App has no user accounts, no login, no email sign-up, and no analytics or crash-reporting service of our own. Your scores, your settings, and the counters in section 2 are stored on your device only, and they are never sent to us. Google's advertising software is a separate matter. It is described in section 3.

2. Data stored on your device

To make the game work, the App saves a little information on your device, using its standard app storage (localStorage). This includes:

  • Your best score for each play mode, including your best for each day's daily puzzle
  • Simple play totals shown on your results screen (for example, runs played and longest chain)
  • Your settings: sound, music, haptics (vibration), reduce motion, colour-blind aids, and tap-to-turn
  • Whether you have already seen a first-run tip, so it is not shown again
  • A few small counters about the optional ad: how many times it was offered, accepted, finished, closed early, or unavailable, and how many times the wheel filled. These stay on your device and are not sent anywhere.

Clear the App's data or uninstall it, and all of this is erased.

On some Android devices, the operating system's own backup feature may copy an app's stored data to your Google account if you have device backup turned on. That is a feature of your device and your settings. The App does not send or receive it, and we cannot see it.

On iPhone and iPad, an iCloud or computer backup of your device may likewise include the App's stored data if you have device backup turned on. Again, that is a feature of your device. The App does not send or receive it, and we cannot see it.

3. Ads and Google AdMob

Spokefall can offer one optional ad. When the wheel fills in Classic mode, you can choose "Watch ad" to get fresh rings. An ad never plays unless you choose it. We use Google AdMob (Google LLC) to provide it.

  • The ad software starts when the App starts. On iPhone, iPad and Android, Google's advertising software starts up on your device at every launch, for every player, and may contact Google, even if you never watch an ad. Where the law requires it, Google's consent message (the User Messaging Platform) appears first, and the ad software does not start until your choice is settled.
  • What Google's software may collect. According to Google's own documentation, it can collect and send to Google your IP address (which can show your general location), device and app identifiers (on Android, the advertising ID and app set ID), app launches, taps and video views, and diagnostic and performance information such as launch time, crashes, and energy use. Google uses this to deliver and measure ads, for analytics, and to prevent fraud.
  • Non-personalised ads only. We ask for non-personalised ads. They are still real ads from Google's network. They are just not tailored to your interests. On iPhone and iPad, we never show the "Allow tracking" request, and the App does not request the advertising identifier. Google's software may still use identifiers tied to the app or the developer to deliver and measure ads.
  • Android advertising ID. You control it in your device settings: Settings > Privacy (or Google) > Ads, where you can delete or reset it.
  • Consent choices. Where the law requires a choice (for example the European Economic Area and the UK), you can reopen it any time from Settings > Ad choices in the App.
  • Google's side. Google handles this data under its own terms and privacy policy: policies.google.com/privacy and policies.google.com/technologies/partner-sites.

4. No purchases

There is nothing to buy in Spokefall. It has no in-app purchases and no subscriptions, and the App never asks for payment details. The game is free, and the optional ad in section 3 is how it is supported.

5. Device features

  • Vibration (Android) and haptics (iPhone) — optional, and you can switch them off in Settings.
  • Network access — used by the ad software. The game itself does not need a connection to play.
  • Sound — the App makes its own sound and music. It does not record sound.

We do not ask for your camera, microphone, contacts, photos, precise location, or tracking permission.

6. Age

Spokefall is a puzzle game made for people aged 13 and over. It is not directed to children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect personal information from children under 13. If you believe a child under 13 has sent us personal information, contact us using the details below and we will look into it.

7. Your choices and deleting your data

  • There is no account to delete, and Garoo holds no server-side copy of your data.
  • Android: open Settings > Apps > Spokefall > Storage and choose Clear data, or uninstall the App. Either erases everything stored on your device.
  • iPhone and iPad: delete the App from your device. That erases everything it stored on your device. If you restore a device backup later, the App's data comes back with it.
  • Use the Ad choices row in the App's Settings (shown where the law requires a choice) to review your consent. On Android, use your device's advertising ID settings to reset or delete your ad ID.
  • To ask about data Google holds from ads, use the Google privacy tools linked in section 3.
  • You can turn off sound, music, and haptics at any time in Settings.

8. Changes to this policy

If what the App does with information changes — for example, more ads, personalised ads, purchases, analytics, accounts, or cloud save — we will update this page and its effective date before that version is released.
